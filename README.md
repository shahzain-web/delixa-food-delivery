# DELIXA 🍔🍕✨

### Modern Food Delivery Website

DELIXA is a modern and responsive food delivery website designed to provide a smooth online food-ordering experience.

The project includes restaurant discovery, food categories, special offers, food items, shopping cart functionality, checkout interface, and a user-friendly design suitable for a modern food delivery platform.

---

## 🌟 Features

* 🏠 Modern & responsive homepage
* 🍔 Food categories
* 🏪 Popular restaurants
* 🍕 Food menu
* ⭐ Featured dishes
* 🎁 Special offers & discounts
* 🔍 Food discovery experience
* 🛒 Add to Cart
* ❤️ Wishlist / Favorite items
* 🧾 Order Summary
* 💳 Checkout interface
* 👤 Login / Sign Up UI
* 📍 Delivery information
* 🚚 Delivery-focused interface
* ⭐ Customer testimonials
* 📞 Contact section
* 📱 Responsive design
* ✨ Smooth animations

## 🛒 Food Ordering Flow

```text
Explore Food
      ↓
Choose Category / Restaurant
      ↓
Select Food
      ↓
Add to Cart
      ↓
Review Order
      ↓
Checkout
      ↓
Enter Delivery Information
      ↓
Place Order
```

---

## 🍕 Main Sections

### Food Categories

Users can explore different types of food through organized categories.

### Popular Restaurants

A dedicated section for discovering popular restaurants.

### Special Offers

Discounts and promotional offers are displayed to encourage users to order.

### Food Menu

Food items are presented with images, names, descriptions, and prices.

### Shopping Cart

Users can add food items to their cart and review their selected order.

### Checkout

A dedicated checkout interface provides the final order review and delivery information.
